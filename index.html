HOW TO RUN THIS APP LOCALLY ON YOUR PHONE (મોબાઇલમાં વાપરવાની રીત)

This entire application is 100% self-contained in a single file! You do not need any backend server. Here are the best ways to run and publish it:

1. Run 100% Offline in Phone Browser

Send this index.html file to your Android or iPhone via WhatsApp, Bluetooth, or Google Drive.

Tap to open in Chrome or Safari. It opens instantly, and all data is saved securely in your phone's browser storage.

2. "Add to Home Screen" (App Icon)

When opened in Chrome on phone, tap the 3 dots menu (⋮) in the top right, then select "Add to Home Screen" (હોમ સ્ક્રીન પર ઉમેરો).

It creates an app icon on your home screen and opens full-screen without any address bar, just like a native mobile app!

3. Free 1-Click Online Publishing

To share with your partner or Munim across multiple phones without cables:

• Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop): Drag & drop this index.html file. In 10 seconds you get a free live link like https://padtar-unjha.netlify.app!

• GitHub Pages: Free permanent hosting from your GitHub repository.

📌 HOW IT WORKS (કેવી રીતે કામ કરે છે)

  • Firm & Year Management: Select your active Financial Year (e.g. 2026-2027) and your Firm (e.g. Shreeji Traders, Unjha). You can create multiple firms and switch anytime.
  • Direct Entry of Rate or Total: You can enter Weight and Rate to get RS Total, OR enter Weight and Total Rs directly to let the app automatically calculate the Rate.
  • Auto Market Fee (0.5%) & Commission (1.5%): The app automatically calculates 0.5% Market Fee and 1.5% Commission based on the Purchase Total before expenses, with a one-click apply button.
  • Clean 1kg & 20kg View: Only the essential 1 kg and 20 kg (Mann / મણ) costing rates are shown by default. Click "Show Extra Weights" whenever you wish to inspect 30 kg, 50 kg, or 100 kg.
  • Cash Credit (CC) Interest: Built-in Mandi credit interest calculator with 2-3 interest-free grace days, daily rates, and net amount payable calculation.
